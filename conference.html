Our Businesses

Conference

Full-service conference and summit management — content strategy, speakers, registration, production and hospitality for seamless multi-day events.

What We Do

Conferences are where ideas, leaders and industries meet. AK Eventive manages every layer — from agenda and speaker coordination to AV, registration and guest experience.

We deliver corporate conferences, industry summits, leadership meets and hybrid formats that run on time, look sharp and leave delegates informed and inspired.

With teams across major cities, we handle venues, travel support, content flow and on-site operations with precision.
