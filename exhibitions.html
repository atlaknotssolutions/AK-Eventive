What We Do

Janjatiya Jeevan Utsav (Tribal Life Festival) is a special exhibition organised to celebrate Janajatiya Gaurav Divas (Tribal Pride Day). The event is dedicated to preserving, showcasing and honouring the rich cultural heritage, traditions and lifestyles of India’s indigenous tribal communities.

A key highlight is the “Veerangana Rani Durgavati Chitra Pradarshani” — a dedicated picture gallery that pays tribute to the historical bravery, life and martyrdom of Rani Durgavati, the legendary 16th-century ruling queen of the Gondwana kingdom.

The entrance pavilion is traditionally decorated with marigold garlands, ceremonial leaf hangings and indigenous motifs. Walls display framed posters and portraits of tribal icons, freedom fighters and cultural leaders, alongside traditional tribal artwork and geometric wall paintings, creating an authentic and immersive celebration of tribal pride.
