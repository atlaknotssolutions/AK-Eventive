About Us

Creating unforgettable experiences since 2016 — with passion, precision and pan-India presence.

Our Story

Established in 2016, AK Eventive has grown into one of India’s trusted event management companies with presence across major cities to ensure seamless local coordination and execution.

Over the years we have continuously upgraded our strengths in every aspect of event planning and delivery. We specialise in creating creative and tasteful experiences — corporate events, live concerts, social gatherings, weddings, sports, exhibitions and roadshows — pan India.

Our professional team turns every event into a world-class experience. We aim not just to plan your event, but to build a long-term relationship with every client.

Presence & Reach

Headquartered in Mumbai, AK Eventive operates across India and the UAE, with offices in Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai. Our partner network ensures seamless delivery anywhere in the world.

Trusted by 600+ global brands — the name AK Eventive has become synonymous with innovation and dependability.

Pioneer in Experiential Marketing

As one of the respected experiential marketing companies, AK Eventive has redefined how brands engage with audiences. From grand live events and exhibitions to digital-first launches, we fuse creativity with flawless delivery.

Iconic work includes large-scale activations, automotive expos and high-impact brand experiences across India and the UAE.

Corporate Events & Business Solutions

At its foundation, AK Eventive has always been a trusted corporate event management company, working with global brands to create meaningful experiences that motivate teams, shape culture and celebrate growth.

Brand Activations & Engagement

As a premier brand activation agency in India and UAE, AK Eventive designs consumer journeys that drive measurable engagement — from retail activations and influencer programs to large-scale hybrid events.

Exhibitions & Trade Shows

With extensive experience as exhibition and trade show organisers, AK Eventive delivers world-class platforms for brands to showcase innovation. Strong storytelling and execution make us a preferred partner.

Entertainment Legacy

As a respected entertainment agency, AK Eventive continues to uphold the power of entertainment to inspire and bring people together — from concerts and cultural spectacles to large-scale public activations.
