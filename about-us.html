Our Story

Established in 2016, Atla Knots Eventive has grown into a trusted event management company with presence across major cities to ensure seamless local coordination and execution.

Over the years we have continuously upgraded our strengths in every aspect of event planning and delivery. We specialise in creating creative and tasteful experiences — corporate events, live concerts, social gatherings, weddings, sports, exhibitions and roadshows — pan India.

Our professional team turns every event into a world-class experience. We aim not just to plan your event, but to build a long-term relationship with every client.

Presence & Reach

Headquartered in Bhopal, Atla Knots Eventive operates across India with dedicated coordination teams in major cities. Our network ensures seamless delivery anywhere in the country.

Trusted by brands, government bodies and organisations — the name Atla Knots Eventive stands for innovation and dependability.

Pioneer in Experiential Marketing

As a respected experiential marketing company, Atla Knots Eventive has redefined how brands engage with audiences. From grand live events and exhibitions to digital-first launches, we fuse creativity with flawless delivery.

Iconic work includes large-scale activations, government sammelans and high-impact brand experiences across India.
