— ABOUT US

THINK EXPERIENCES. THINK AK EVENTIVE.

It began in 1990 — three visionaries, one bold mission, and a belief that creativity could shape extraordinary experiences.

From a small office with big ambition, AK Eventive grew into one of India's foremost event management companies, partnering with global brands, government bodies, Fortune 500 companies and cultural institutions.

Today we stand as a full-spectrum experiential events company — spanning corporate conferences, product launches, cultural showcases, global roadshows, sports ceremonies and destination weddings — with teams across Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Dubai.
