Our Businesses

Event Management

End-to-end planning, production and execution of corporate events, conferences, product launches, concerts and large-scale experiences across India and beyond.

What We Do

AK Eventive delivers complete event management solutions — from concept and creative direction to on-ground execution and post-event reporting.

Whether it is a high-stakes corporate conference, a brand launch, a multi-city roadshow or a large public spectacle, our team manages every detail so you can focus on your guests and your goals.

With teams in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Dubai, we bring local expertise and national scale to every project.
