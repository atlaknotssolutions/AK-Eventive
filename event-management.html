Event Management

End-to-end planning, production and execution of corporate events, conferences, product launches, concerts and large-scale experiences across India and beyond.

What We Do

AK Eventive: Executing India’s Most Impactful Public Milestones

At AK Eventive, we transform visionary public initiatives into flawless reality. We deliver comprehensive, end-to-end event management solutions for high-profile government and public programs — from strategic concept and creative direction to seamless on-ground execution and detailed post-event reporting.

Scale That Matches National Ambition
We specialize in orchestrating mega-scale public events that define regional and national growth. Our portfolio includes managing massive development portfolios (groundbreaking ceremonies for multi-sector projects exceeding ₹19,000 crore), large-scale beneficiary programs (Mass Grih Pravesh under PMAY for over 2.21 lakh families), national infrastructure launches (National Highway networks and new rail connectivity lines), and public utility initiatives (foundation stone ceremonies under the Jal Jeevan Mission).
